Ameer 070 is a bilingual Arabic + Latin sans-serif family in four weights (Light, Regular, Bold, Black). It is drawn as a calm monoline with soft, hexagon-inspired corners and near-round soft-hexagon dots, and was balanced letter by letter against the proportions of contemporary Arabic sans designs so that it reads clearly in interfaces and headlines alike. Latin figures share one width, which keeps timing and pace tables aligned.

The family covers Arabic and Latin (including Western European accented letters) with contextual Arabic shaping, mark positioning and kerning.
