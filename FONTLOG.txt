FONTLOG for Ameer 070
=====================

Basic Font Information
----------------------
Ameer 070 is a bilingual Arabic/Latin sans-serif family in four static weights: Light (300), Regular (400), Bold (700), Black (900).

ChangeLog
---------
2026-11-20 (v2.000)
- Arabic letters redrawn as a monoline system with soft hexagon-inspired joins; every letter balanced against
  reference proportions (top/bottom and left/right rulers), in all four weights.
- New soft-hexagon dots with weight-aware spacing; hamza family adapted from Alexandria (OFL) and matched to each weight's stroke.
- Latin a-z, A-Z, figures, accented letters and symbols redrawn/rebuilt on the new metrics; new optical kerning.
- Added symbols: prime/double prime, arrows, approx/relations, per mille, Rial sign, thin/figure/hair spaces.
- Collision/spacing pass across all Arabic letter pairs; mark anchors regenerated from the new outlines.

2026 (v1.200)
- Initial release.
